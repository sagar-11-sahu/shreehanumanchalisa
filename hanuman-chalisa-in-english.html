Complete Hanuman Chalisa in Roman English

Doha
Shri Guru Charan Saroj Raj, Nij Manu Mukur Sudhari.
Baranau Raghubar Bimal Jasu, Jo Dayaku Phal Chari.

Buddhiheen Tanu Janike, Sumirau Pavan Kumar.
Bal Buddhi Vidya Dehu Mohi, Harahu Kalesh Vikar.

Jai Hanuman Gyan Gun Sagar,
Jai Kapis Tihun Lok Ujagar.

Anjani Putra Pavan Sut Nama.
Chaupai 3
Mahavir Vikram Bajrangi,
Kumati Nivar Sumati Ke Sangi.
Chaupai 4
Kanchan Baran Biraj Subesa,
Kanan Kundal Kunchit Kesa.
Chaupai 5
Hath Bajra Aur Dhwaja Birajai,
Kandhe Moonj Janeu Sajai.

Chaupai 7
Vidyavan Guni Ati Chatur,
Ram Kaj Karibe Ko Atur.
Chaupai 8

Ram Lakhan Sita Man Basiya.
Chaupai 9
Sukshma Roop Dhari Siyahi Dikhawa,
Bikat Roop Dhari Lank Jarawa.
Chaupai 10
Bhim Roop Dhari Asur Sanhare,
Ramachandra Ke Kaj Sanware.
Chaupai 11
Laye Sajivan Lakhan Jiyaye,
Shri Raghubir Harashi Ur Laye.
Chaupai 12
Raghupati Kinhi Bahut Badai,
Tum Mam Priya Bharat Hi Sam Bhai.

Prabhu Mudrika Meli Mukh Mahi,
Jaladhi Langhi Gaye Acharaj Nahi.
Chaupai 20
Durgam Kaj Jagat Ke Jete,
Sugam Anugrah Tumhare Tete.

Chaupai 31

Sada Raho Raghupati Ke Dasa.

Chaupai 34
Ant Kaal Raghubar Pur Jai,
Jahan Janma Hari Bhakt Kahai.

Aur Devta Chitt Na Dharai,

Chhutahi Bandi Maha Sukh Hoi.

Hoye Siddhi Sakhi Gaurisa.

Keejai Nath Hriday Mah Dera.

Pavan Tanay Sankat Haran, Mangal Murti Roop.
Ram Lakhan Sita Sahit, Hriday Basahu Sur Bhoop.

Hanuman Chalisa Meaning in English

Opening Doha: The devotee remembers the Guru and asks for wisdom, knowledge, strength and freedom from suffering.
1: Hanuman is praised as an ocean of knowledge and virtues whose glory illuminates the three worlds.
2: Hanuman is described as Lord Rama's messenger, immensely powerful, and the son of Anjana and the Wind God.
3: Hanuman is praised for courage and strength and for removing wrong thinking while supporting wisdom.
4: His radiant appearance, golden complexion, earrings and curly hair are described.
5: He is depicted carrying the thunderbolt and flag, with the sacred thread on his shoulder.
6: Hanuman is praised as the son of Kesari and as possessing extraordinary divine glory.
7: He is knowledgeable, virtuous and intelligent, always eager to perform Lord Rama's work.
8: Hanuman delights in hearing Lord Rama's story, while Rama, Lakshmana and Sita remain in his heart.
9: He took a small form before Sita and later assumed a formidable form to burn Lanka.
10: He assumed a powerful form to defeat demons and complete Lord Rama's mission.
11: Hanuman brought the Sanjeevani herb and revived Lakshmana, bringing joy to Lord Rama.
12: Lord Rama praised Hanuman greatly and said he was as dear to him as his brother Bharata.
13: Rama said Hanuman's glory could be praised by thousands of mouths and embraced him with affection.
14: Great sages and divine beings are associated with praising Hanuman's greatness.
15: Even great celestial beings and learned poets cannot fully describe Hanuman's glory.
16: Hanuman helped Sugriva meet Lord Rama, after which Sugriva regained his kingdom.
17: Vibhishana followed Hanuman's counsel and eventually became ruler of Lanka.
18: Hanuman is described as leaping toward the sun, which appeared to him like a sweet fruit.
19: Carrying Rama's ring, Hanuman crossed the ocean; for him, such a feat was not impossible.
20: Difficult tasks are described as becoming easier through Hanuman's grace.
21: Hanuman is traditionally regarded as guardian at the door of Lord Rama.
22: Those who seek Hanuman's refuge are described as receiving comfort and protection.
23: Hanuman possesses immense power, and his mighty presence is said to inspire awe throughout the three worlds.
24: This expresses the traditional devotional belief that negative spiritual forces stay away when Hanuman's name is remembered.
25: This expresses the traditional devotional belief that remembering Hanuman brings relief from illness and suffering; it is not a substitute for medical care.
26: Those who remember Hanuman sincerely in thought, action and speech are described as receiving help through difficulties.
27: Hanuman is praised for carrying out the tasks of Lord Rama, described as the supreme king devoted to spiritual discipline.
28: The verse expresses the devotional belief that sincere prayers and wishes offered with devotion receive abundant blessings.
29: Hanuman's glory is described as being renowned throughout all four ages.
30: Hanuman is praised as protector of saints and devotees and beloved servant of Lord Rama.
31: Hanuman is traditionally described as the giver of eight spiritual powers and nine forms of treasure.
32: Hanuman possesses the spiritual essence of devotion to Rama and remains eternally devoted to him.
33: Devotion to Hanuman is described as a path toward devotion to Lord Rama and freedom from worldly sorrow.
34: The verse expresses a traditional devotional hope of reaching Rama's abode and remaining connected with divine devotion.
35: The devotee expresses exclusive devotion to Hanuman and seeks the happiness associated with his worship.
36: Remembering powerful Hanuman is traditionally believed to bring relief from difficulties and suffering.
37: Hanuman is praised repeatedly, with a prayer asking him to show grace like a revered teacher.
38: The verse traditionally promises spiritual benefit and relief from bondage to one who recites the Chalisa repeatedly with devotion.
39: The verse describes the Hanuman Chalisa as capable of bringing spiritual accomplishment and invokes Lord Shiva as witness.
40: Tulsidas identifies himself as a lifelong servant of God and asks Hanuman to reside in his heart.
Closing Doha: The closing prayer asks Hanuman, the son of the Wind God and remover of difficulties, to remain in the heart together with Rama, Lakshmana and Sita.

About Hanuman Chalisa

The Hanuman Chalisa is a forty-verse devotional hymn traditionally attributed to Goswami Tulsidas and dedicated to Lord Hanuman. This page is for readers who prefer the English alphabet and want to understand the traditional devotional meaning.
